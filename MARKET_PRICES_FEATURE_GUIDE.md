# Enhanced Market Prices Feature - User Guide

## Overview
The Market Prices feature has been significantly enhanced with powerful search, filter, and comparison capabilities to help farmers make informed selling decisions.

## Key Features

### 1. **Three Main Tabs**

#### **My Crop Tab**
- View today's price for your currently planted crop
- 6-month price trend chart with historical data
- AI-powered price predictions for:
  - Next week
  - Next month
  - Harvest season
- Nearby mandi/market prices in your region
- Government MSP (Minimum Support Price) information
- Smart selling strategy recommendations

#### **Other Crops Tab**
- Browse prices of other crops (Wheat, Soybean, Sugarcane, Tomato, Onion, etc.)
- Compare demand levels (High/Medium/Low)
- View price trends (+/-%)
- **NEW: Compare Button** - Click to compare any crop with your current crop
- Get recommendations for high-demand crops for next season

#### **Search Prices Tab** 🔍 **NEW!**
- Advanced search and filter functionality
- Filter by:
  - **Crop Name**: Select from 20+ major crops
  - **State**: Choose from 14 Indian states
  - **Market/Mandi**: Select specific mandis in the chosen state
- Real-time search results with:
  - Current price per quintal
  - Trend direction (+/-%)
  - Demand level
  - MSP (where applicable)
  - Location details (Market, State)

### 2. **Crop Comparison Feature** 📊 **NEW!**

When you click the "Compare" button on any crop, you'll see:

**Side-by-Side Comparison Card:**
- **Your Crop** (Left side - Green background)
  - Crop name
  - Current price
  - Demand level
  - Price trend
  
- **Selected Crop** (Right side - Blue background)
  - Crop name
  - Current price
  - Demand level
  - Price trend

**Price Difference Analysis:**
- Clear indication if selected crop is higher/lower in price
- Exact price difference in rupees
- Visual indicators (up/down arrows)

**Comparison Insights:**
- Demand level comparison
- Profitability analysis
- Recommendations based on data

### 3. **How to Use**

#### Finding Today's Price for Any Crop:
1. Go to Market tab → Market Prices
2. Click on "Search Prices" tab
3. Select:
   - Crop (e.g., Cotton, Wheat, Onion)
   - State (e.g., Maharashtra, Punjab)
   - Market/Mandi (optional - for specific market)
4. Click "Apply Filters"
5. View results with prices, trends, and demand

#### Comparing Crops:
1. Navigate to "Other Crops" tab OR "Search Prices" tab
2. Find the crop you want to compare
3. Click the purple "Compare with [Your Crop]" button
4. Review the comparison card that appears at the top
5. Make informed decisions based on:
   - Price difference
   - Demand levels
   - Market trends
6. Click "Close Comparison" when done

### 4. **Search Filters Explained**

**Crop Filter:**
- Choose from: Cotton, Wheat, Soybean, Rice, Onion, Tomato, Potato, Maize, Bajra, Jowar, Groundnut, Sunflower, Chilli, Turmeric, Garlic, Gram, Tur, Moong, Urad, and more

**State Filter:**
- Available states: Maharashtra, Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, West Bengal, Bihar, Odisha

**Market Filter:**
- Automatically populates based on selected state
- Shows APMC markets, mandis, and local markets

### 5. **Data Insights**

Each search result shows:
- **Price**: Current rate per quintal (₹)
- **Trend**: Percentage change from last month (+12%, -3%, etc.)
- **Demand Badge**: 
  - 🟢 High (Green) - Strong market demand
  - 🟡 Medium (Yellow) - Moderate demand
  - ⚫ Low (Gray) - Weak demand
- **MSP**: Government Minimum Support Price (if applicable)
- **Location**: Exact market and state

## Example Use Cases

### Use Case 1: Finding Best Price for Today
**Scenario**: You have Cotton ready to sell today
**Steps**:
1. Go to Search Prices tab
2. Select "Cotton" as crop
3. Select "Maharashtra" as state
4. Click "Apply Filters"
5. Review all Cotton prices across Maharashtra markets
6. Choose the market with highest price (e.g., Latur Market ₹6,320)

### Use Case 2: Planning Next Season
**Scenario**: Deciding between Wheat and Soybean for next planting
**Steps**:
1. Go to Other Crops tab
2. Click "Compare" on Wheat
3. Review: Wheat ₹2,150, High demand, +8% trend
4. Close comparison
5. Click "Compare" on Soybean
6. Review: Soybean ₹4,200, High demand, +12% trend
7. Decision: Soybean offers better price and stronger trend

### Use Case 3: Cross-State Price Check
**Scenario**: Checking if selling in another state is profitable
**Steps**:
1. Go to Search Prices
2. Select your crop (e.g., "Rice")
3. Try different states:
   - Punjab: ₹2,100
   - Haryana: ₹2,050
   - West Bengal: ₹1,980
4. Identify Punjab offers best rate
5. Consider transport costs for final decision

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Regular Monitoring**: Check prices weekly to track trends
2. **Compare Multiple Markets**: Don't settle for first result
3. **Consider MSP**: Always ensure price is above MSP
4. **Follow AI Predictions**: Use forecasts for timing sales
5. **Demand Matters**: High demand crops = better negotiation power
6. **Seasonal Variations**: Prices fluctuate with harvest seasons
7. **Transport Costs**: Factor in distance when comparing markets
